# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to make participation welcoming and constructive. Be respectful, accept well-reasoned feedback, focus disagreement on ideas, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or sharing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's security contact or maintainer profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the community.
